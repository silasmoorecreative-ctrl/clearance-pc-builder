        About

        Clearance

        Clearance shows a PC build in 3D before you buy it. Pick a case and parts, and it checks every fit with the dimensions each maker publishes: card length, cooler height, radiator mounts, power supply size and wattage.

        It also checks the build as laid out: if any two solid parts would occupy the same space, the fit check says which ones and by how much, and a red box marks the spot. Cables and liquid-cooler tubes bend around parts on their own. Zoom in close to the motherboard to see every connector named, or view any part on its own from the parts list.

        Guided build walks through assembly in an order that suits your parts, zooming to each connector and naming the label printed beside it. Each step has a short checklist that saves in this browser, the screws it needs, and the one mistake to watch for.

        • Orbit (default): drag to turn, ← → ↑ ↓ with the view focused, hold Space and drag to pan. View has turn and zoom buttons too.
        • Roam: aim with the mouse, W A S D to move, E up, Q down, Shift faster, arrow keys to turn, Esc to free the mouse
        • Zoom: scroll or pinch, or + −
        • Reset the view: 0
        • Close a panel: Esc

        Parts are modeled at true scale from published specs and drawings. Each case, board, card, cooler, memory kit and power supply copies its own layout, shape and finish from manuals and review photos, with logos and printed names left off. Some heights and positions are estimated from photos, so the manual is the final word on exact spots.
